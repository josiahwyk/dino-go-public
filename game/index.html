Grown-up settings

This week's spelling words

Type the words from the homework book, separated by spaces or commas. They spell one to catch each dino.

Need ideas? Suggest words

Pick a spelling pattern, then tap Suggest words. It replaces the box above with about 8 words from a built-in list (no internet needed). Change them if you like, then tap Save words. Tap Suggest again for a different set.

Challenge words are a separate setting. Suggest words only fills in this week's list. Challenge words are Year 1 extras the game mixes in by itself: about 1 catch in 4 uses one instead of a word from your list (blends like "frog", long vowels like "rain", word families like "played", two-syllable words like "rabbit"). Suggest words never offers those.

Maths in battles

Questions follow the Australian Curriculum (v9). Levels 1–6 are Year 1 and levels 7–12 are Year 2. It moves up a level by itself after 5 right first time in a row, and back down if they're stuck twice. About 1 in 5 questions reviews an easier level.

Battle questions

What they answer to attack in a battle. Spelling words uses this week's words (plus challenge words if they're on): the easy move fills in one missing letter, the medium move two letters, and the hard move picks the right spelling out of four. Mix asks maths and spelling about half each.

Glow the right answer

After a few wrong tries, the right maths answer (after 2 wrong taps) or the right spelling letter (after 3 wrong taps) can glow so they can find it. Off by default. Spelling still says the word again either way.

Battle length

How many right answers it takes to beat a wild dino.
